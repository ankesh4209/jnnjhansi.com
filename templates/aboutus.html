City Overview & Urban Growth

Jhansi, situated between the rivers Pahunj and Betwa, is a symbol of courage, self-respect, and patriotic valour. Under Raja Bir Singh Deo in 1613, the historic Jhansi Fort was constructed. During the 1857 First War of Independence, Maharani Lakshmibai immortalized Jhansi across the globe with her legendary bravery.

Today, Jhansi Nagar Nigam administers a rapidly progressing smart city spanning over 120+ wards, dedicated to solid waste management, drinking water distribution, street lighting, modern parks, roads, and transparent e-governance for over 4.25 Lakh citizens.
